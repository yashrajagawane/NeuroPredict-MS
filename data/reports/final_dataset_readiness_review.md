# Final Dataset Readiness Review

## Recommendation

**NO-GO: do not begin Hybrid SimpleRNN-LSTM training.**

The legal public-source acquisition and audit are complete. A scientifically
defensible longitudinal CIS-to-MS training dataset is not yet available. This
decision prevents fabricated temporal sequences, unvalidated labels, and
follow-up leakage.

## Inclusion/exclusion rule for a future approved cohort

Include a patient only when all of the following can be verified: baseline CIS
at/preceding the index, pseudonymous patient linkage, pre-index dated
observations, an approved outcome criterion/version, and adequate outcome
follow-up.

Exclude or separately retain records with established MS before index, an
unresolvable identity/linkage problem, missing/ambiguous index, impossible
chronology, unconfirmed conversion, or insufficient follow-up for a negative
label. Unknown/censored records are not negative labels.

## Data-quality and leakage conclusion

The current files fail the core temporal requirements. There are no visit
dates, conversion dates, or last-follow-up dates; a Mexican duplicate source
identifier needs source-owner review; categorical codebooks are incomplete;
and Mexican final EDSS is an explicit post-baseline leakage risk. The
Lithuanian file also has an adult-eligibility inconsistency and high missingness
for several potential MRI/CSF fields.

## Conditions to change this decision

Reconsider training only after an approved cohort passes the longitudinal
schema and label-validation gates, including patient-level date linkage,
verified conversion and follow-up dates, a fixed predeclared horizon, source
feature definitions, leakage review, and patient-level evaluation splitting.
